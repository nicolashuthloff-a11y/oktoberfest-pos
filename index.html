Oktoberfest Menu & Food Pairings

Official serving guide: which sausage pairs with which roll, sauce & side

​
​
🌭 Bratwurst (140g Traditional White Sausage)
R60.00
🥖 Best Roll Pairing German Crispy / Crusty Roll

Firm crust holds the 140g thick-cut sausage without soaking through.

🥫 Recommended Sauces Sweet or Medium German Mustard

Classic Bavarian Senf. Tomato sauce available for children.

🥗 Ideal Sides Warm Sauerkraut (+R15)

Spoon directly on top of sausage in the crispy roll.

​
🍛 Currywurst (140g Sliced Bratwurst in Curry Glaze)
R70.00
🥖 Roll on the Side German Crispy Roll (On Side)

Dunk the crusty bread into the rich spiced curry-tomato sauce.

🥣 Sauce & Plating Warm Curry-Tomato + Curry Dust

Served sliced in a cardboard boat/tray with wooden fork/skewer.

🥨 Alternate Pairing Extra Bread Roll (+R15)

Many guests order a 2nd roll to clean out the curry glaze.

​
🥩 Bockwurst (140g Smoked Beechwood Sausage)
R60.00
🥖 Best Roll Pairing German Crispy / Crusty Roll

Smoky pork & beef snap is complemented by crunchy bakery bread.

🥫 Recommended Sauces Scharfer Senf (Hot Mustard)

Sharper spicy mustard cuts the rich smoke flavours.

🥗 Alternate Roll White Roll (If requested)

Soft roll option is available if customers prefer a milder bite.

​
🧀 Cheesegriller (110g Melted Cheddar Sausage)
R60.00
🥖 Best Roll Pairing Standard White / Hot Dog Roll

Soft white roll catches the hot melted cheddar cheese pockets.

🥫 Recommended Sauces Sweet Tomato Sauce / Ketchup

Classic cheesy braai combination loved across South Africa.

🥨 Alternate Bun German Crispy Roll

Available for customers who love extra crunch.
